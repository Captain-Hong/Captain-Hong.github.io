Biography

Born in 1991, Jin Hong received his Ph.D. degree from Sun Yat-sen University, and was a joint Ph.D. student at the University of Leicester (UK) supervised by Prof. Yudong Zhang (Changjiang Scholar). He was a postdoctoral fellow at The Chinese University of Hong Kong supervised by Prof. Weitian Chen. He is a member of Sigma Xi, The Scientific Research Honor Society (since 2023). Currently, he is an Associate Professor at the School of Artificial Intelligence, Nanchang University.

He has hosted 1 National Natural Science Foundation of China (NSFC) project and 1 Jiangxi Provincial Natural Science Foundation project, and participated in 3 NSFC projects. He has published over 40 papers in journals and conferences, including 23 as first/corresponding author in SCI journals such as Knowledge-Based Systems, Applied Soft Computing, Neural Networks, IEEE Transactions on Consumer Electronics, Computers in Biology and Medicine, etc. His Google Scholar citations exceed 1318, with an H-index of 18.
